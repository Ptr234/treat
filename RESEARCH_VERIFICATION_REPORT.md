### 3. Tax Information Verification ✅

**Uganda Revenue Authority Tax Rates (2024):**

**Individual Income Tax:**
- Tax-free threshold: UGX 3.18M annually
- Tax brackets: 0%, 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%
- Source: URA Tax Guide 2024

**Corporate Tax:**
- Standard rate: 30%
- Small business rate: 20% (turnover < UGX 150M)
- Mining/Telecom: 30%

**VAT:**
- Standard rate: 18%
- Registration threshold: UGX 150M annually
